01

What I do

I design and ship intelligent workflow automation — n8n, Python, AI agents and REST APIs wired into the systems a business already runs on.

Most of my work replaces a chain of manual handoffs (a form, an inbox, a spreadsheet, a Slack message) with one pipeline that reads, reasons and routes on its own.

My background is enterprise IT, not a bootcamp: banking, construction, education and real-estate operations, where I managed the servers, the ERP and the people asking why the printer is down. That's the part most automation builders skip — and it's why the workflows I ship account for the messy exceptions, not just the happy path.

Process analysis

I map how work actually flows through a team, find where it stalls, and price the automation before writing a single node.

Pipeline engineering

n8n, Python, webhooks and OAuth integrations — built with retries, duplicate guards and logging so they survive production.

AI that does work

Agents, RAG and OCR wired into real business logic — not a chatbot bolted onto the side of a process.
